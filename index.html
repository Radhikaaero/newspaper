About

newsdatewithrads

newsdatewithrads is an independent, hobbyist project that explains the UN Sustainable Development Goals for everyday readers. It comes from a background in aerospace engineering and a long-running interest in sustainability, technology and space. Everything is built on original research and links to its sources so every claim can be checked.
